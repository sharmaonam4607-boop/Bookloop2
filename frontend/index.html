How BookLoop Works

Every book has a lifecycle — giving it a second life in 4 simple steps

1

List in 60 Seconds

Enter textbook title, condition, and choose whether to sell, swap, donate, or lend for the semester.

2

Discover Nearby

Students in your college or adjacent campuses find your listing through search and distance filters.

3

Safe Campus Handoff

Chat directly, agree on terms, and meet at designated campus meeting points (Library, Canteen, Student Center).

4

Build Trust & Impact

Rate your interaction to grow community trust score while tracking your environmental paper-saving impact.
